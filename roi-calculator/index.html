Parameter notes

Quartz and Titan use different assumptions.

SolarEX Quartz — SiO₂

Passive, UV-independent SiO₂ surface architecture for hydrophobic and oleophobic easy-clean behavior. Default model: Europe +10% / 5 years; Middle East +2% / 3 years.

  • No UV activation required
  • Best aligned with dust, pollen, salt and mineral soiling
  • Use site conditions and cleaning records for final review

SolarEX Titan — TiO₂

Active TiO₂ photocatalytic pathway for UV-supported organic-contaminant decomposition and hydrophilic rinse behavior. Default model: +5.15% from 360-day study reference / 5 years.

  • UV activation required
  • Best aligned with organic, biological or atmospheric fouling
  • Confirm UV availability during technical review
