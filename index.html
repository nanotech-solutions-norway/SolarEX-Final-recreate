Core platform

Two pathways. Different mechanisms. One surface-engineering platform.

SolarEX is not a generic coating category. Quartz and Titan are distinct mechanisms with different activation requirements and different best-fit contamination profiles.

SolarEX Quartz — passive SiO₂

Quartz forms a transparent 100–150 nm SiO₂ nano-scale layer. It modifies surface energy to create hydrophobic and oleophobic easy-clean behavior without requiring UV activation.

  • Primary fit: dust, pollen, bird lime, salt, mineral particulates and grime.
  • Manual consumption: 15–17 mL/m².
  • Industrial route: 15–20 mL/m².
  • Ideal application temperature: 15–20°C.
Open Quartz page

SolarEX Titan — active TiO₂

Titan forms a TiO₂ nanoparticle layer that activates under natural sunlight. The UV-supported photocatalytic pathway generates hydrophilic sheeting and targets organic/biological contamination.

  • Primary fit: organic, biological and atmospheric contamination.
  • Typical consumption: 10–25 mL/m².
  • Wipe/polish range: 10–15 mL/m².
  • Full outdoor effect: 24–48 hours under sunlight.

How SolarEX works

From site assessment to evidence review.

The frontpage now follows the report’s recommended explainer logic: short, progressive and operational.

Assess the site

Review location, UV availability, contamination profile, water availability, cleaning burden, module type and commercial objective.

2

Select pathway

Use Quartz for passive, UV-independent SiO₂ surface energy control. Use Titan where UV-supported TiO₂ photocatalysis is technically appropriate.

Prepare and apply

Apply according to product-specific cleaning, coverage, temperature, hardening and cure requirements.

4

Monitor and evaluate

Compare treated and control areas using baseline yield, cleaning log, soiling profile and interval monitoring.
