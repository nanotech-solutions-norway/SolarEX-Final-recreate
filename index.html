Operational problem

PV soiling creates yield uncertainty, cleaning pressure and avoidable O&M exposure.

Dust, salt, pollen, bird lime, organic films and biological fouling can reduce optical transmission and make surface recovery more costly. SolarEX addresses the problem through site-specific surface engineering rather than one-size-fits-all coating selection.

Pathway selector

Quartz and Titan are not interchangeable.

The correct route depends on UV availability, dominant contamination type, rainfall pattern, cleaning method, water availability and commercial objective. Use pathway review to determine whether Quartz, Titan, both, further testing or no coating route is the appropriate next step.

SolarEX Quartz — passive SiO₂

Quartz forms a transparent SiO₂ nano-scale surface architecture for UV-independent easy-clean behavior. It is the default review route where mineral/inorganic soiling, dust, sand, salt, pollen or cleaning burden dominates.

  • Best initial fit: low-UV, high-latitude, rainfall-assisted and mineral-soiling environments.
  • Commercial angle: cleaning optimization, reduced adhesion, water/labor review and easier surface recovery.
  • CTA: request Quartz ROI screen or application pack.
Open Quartz pathway

SolarEX Titan — active TiO₂

Titan forms a TiO₂ nanoparticle pathway that activates under suitable sunlight. It is reviewed where organic, biological, smog-derived or atmospheric contamination makes active photocatalysis technically relevant.

  • Best initial fit: UV-sufficient sites with organic or atmospheric fouling.
  • Commercial angle: active contaminant decomposition, hydrophilic rinse behavior and evidence-led validation.
  • CTA: validate Titan fit for UV and contamination profile.
