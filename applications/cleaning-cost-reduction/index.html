Cost drivers

Cleaning cost is more than wash frequency.

Water use

Water availability, water price and logistics can dominate the O&M case in dry or remote environments.

Labor and access

Truck-rolls, rooftop access, labor scheduling and downtime affect practical cleaning economics.

Abrasion risk

Repeated or aggressive cleaning can create surface-wear concerns that should be considered in pilot design.

Yield recovery

The commercial case depends on measured recovery, energy value, cleaning interval and soiling rate.
