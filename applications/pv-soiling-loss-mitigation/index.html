Application

PV soiling loss mitigation with pathway-selected surface engineering.

PV soiling reduces optical transmission, increases cleaning burden and introduces uncertainty in production forecasting. SolarEX supports mitigation through site-specific selection between passive SiO₂ Quartz and active TiO₂ Titan.

The correct recommendation depends on contamination profile, UV availability, rainfall, cleaning model, water access, application quality and pilot evidence.

Problem context

Soiling is not one problem.

Mineral dust and sand

Often favors a Quartz-first review where passive adhesion reduction and easy-clean behavior are the commercial priority.

Salt, pollen and bird lime

Requires site review of adhesion, rainfall support, cleaning method, residue risk and abrasion exposure.

Organic and biological films

May favor Titan if the site has suitable UV exposure and the contamination profile supports active TiO₂ photocatalysis.

Mixed contamination

Requires a pathway recommendation based on dominant fouling mechanism and pilot validation design.

Decision route

From soiling profile to coating pathway.

1

Map the soiling source

Classify mineral, salt, pollen, organic, biological, industrial or mixed contamination.

2

Review UV and rainfall

Use UV availability and rinse behavior to decide whether passive or active surface logic is defensible.

3

Select Quartz, Titan or further review

Quartz is UV-independent. Titan requires suitable UV exposure and relevant organic/atmospheric fouling.

4

Validate with treated/control evidence

Use baseline, matched controls, cleaning logs and interval monitoring before scale-up.
