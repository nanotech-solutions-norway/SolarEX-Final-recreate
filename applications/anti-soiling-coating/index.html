Quartz vs Titan for anti-soiling

Anti-soiling performance depends on mechanism fit.

Decision factorQuartz SiO₂Titan TiO₂
MechanismPassive surface-energy modification and easy-clean behavior.UV-supported photocatalysis and hydrophilic rinse behavior.
Primary fitDust, sand, salt, pollen, bird lime, mineral particulates and grime.Organic film, biological fouling, smog-derived and atmospheric residues.

Buyer questions

Anti-soiling coating should answer a site problem, not a generic coating preference.

Self-cleaning?

SolarEX supports easier recovery and route-specific soiling mitigation. It should not be positioned as cleaning elimination.

Cleaning reduction?

Reduction potential depends on soiling rate, cleaning method, water access, rainfall and pilot evidence.

UV dependency?

Quartz is UV-independent. Titan requires suitable UV exposure to activate TiO₂ photocatalysis.

ROI proof?

Use ROI scenarios and treated/control pilots before large-scale procurement decisions.
