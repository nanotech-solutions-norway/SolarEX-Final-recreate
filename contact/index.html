What to include

Information that improves response quality.

Site conditions

Country, region, climate zone, UV profile, altitude, proximity to dust, salt, agriculture, traffic or industrial sources.

Contamination profile

Dust, pollen, bird lime, mineral residue, salt, organic film, biological fouling, smog or mixed contamination.

Maintenance context

Cleaning frequency, cleaning chemistry, water availability, labor cost, access constraints and current O&M pain points.

Project scope

Module count, m² glass area, pilot area, product interest, decision timeline and commercial objective.

Initial pathway guidance

Initial routing before technical review.

Quartz indicative profile

  • Dust, pollen, salt or mineral particulate fouling.
  • Lower UV index or high-latitude location.
  • Rainfall-assisted cleaning potential.
  • Primary objective: passive adhesion reduction and easier cleaning.

Titan indicative profile

  • Organic, biological or atmospheric contamination.
  • Sufficient UV availability for photocatalytic activation.
  • Industrial or smog-derived surface films.
  • Primary objective: active decomposition and hydrophilic rinse behavior.
