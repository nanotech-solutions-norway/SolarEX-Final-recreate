What to include

Information that improves response quality.

Site conditions

Country, region, climate zone, UV profile and exposure to dust, salt, agriculture, traffic or industrial sources.

Contamination profile

Dust, pollen, bird lime, mineral residue, salt, organic film, biological fouling, smog or mixed contamination.

Maintenance context

Cleaning frequency, cleaning chemistry, water availability, labor cost and current O&M pain points.

Project scope

Module count, m² glass area, pilot area, product interest, decision timeline and commercial objective.
