First response quality

What to include in your inquiry.

Site conditions

Geographic location, climate zone, irradiance and UV index context, altitude and terrain profile.

Contamination profile

Primary soiling type: dust, pollen, organic, industrial or biological; seasonal fouling patterns; proximity to pollution sources.

Maintenance context

Current cleaning frequency, water availability and usage volume, existing O&M protocols and access constraints.

Project scope

Module count, installation type, coating interest, performance goals and pilot timing.

Initial guidance

Initial pathway guidance.

Quartz indicative profile

  • Dust, pollen or mineral particulate fouling
  • Rainfall-assisted cleaning potential
  • Lower UV index or high-latitude location
  • Primarily inorganic contamination sources

Titan indicative profile

  • Organic or biological fouling
  • Smog or industrial atmospheric contamination
  • Sufficient UV availability for photocatalytic activation
  • Complex mixed-contamination environments
