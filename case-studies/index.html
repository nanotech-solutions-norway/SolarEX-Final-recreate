Monitored Study

Titan rooftop study context

Method: Titan TiO₂ pathway, 63 coated modules, 360 monitored days and 15-minute interval monitoring.

Result: +5.15% average uplift in source study context, with string-level values of +5.62%, +5.22% and +4.62%.

Next step: confirm UV availability and organic/atmospheric contamination fit before local pilot design.
