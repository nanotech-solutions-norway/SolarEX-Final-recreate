Claims discipline

Partner sales must remain evidence-led.

No guarantees

Avoid universal yield, payback or maintenance-free claims.

Use evidence labels

Separate monitored study, ROI scenario, technical parameter and pilot hypothesis.

Route by mechanism

Quartz and Titan are not interchangeable product names.

Validate locally

Material projects should use site-fit review and pilot validation before scale-up.
