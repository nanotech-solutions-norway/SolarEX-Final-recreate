Engineering principle

Select the coating pathway that matches your PV site, contamination profile and O&M objective.

When the pathway is correct, SolarEX can support easier surface recovery, clearer pilot measurement and a defensible ROI screen.

1. Identify soiling

Classify dust, salt, pollen, mineral, organic, biological or mixed atmospheric deposits.

2. Check UV

Quartz does not need UV. Titan requires UV to activate photocatalysis.

3. Define cleaning objective

Water, labor, access, abrasion and surface recovery shape the commercial route.

4. Validate pilot design

Use matched treated/control areas, baseline data, cleaning logs and monitoring interval.

Request a pathway recommendation.

Submit site location, contamination profile, UV context, cleaning method, module area and project objective. SolarEX will route the site to Quartz, Titan, both, further review or pilot validation.
