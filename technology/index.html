Surface science

Surface Science for Solar Assets

SolarEX technology is built around two distinct nanocoating pathways for photovoltaic glass: passive SiO₂ repellence and active TiO₂ photocatalysis. Product selection is an engineering decision based on UV availability, contamination profile and operating environment.

Engineering principle

Surface coating selection is not a commodity choice.

The mechanism by which a coating modifies glass behavior must match the contamination profile, UV availability and O&M logic of each site.

Optical interface protection

The glass-air interface is where irradiance is lost to soiling. Nanocoating modifies this interface at molecular level while preserving optical clarity.

Adhesion-energy control

Surface energy determines how contaminants bond to glass. SiO₂ and TiO₂ alter this behavior through different routes.

Environment-matched mechanism

Contamination profiles vary by geography, climate and industrial context. Mechanism fit determines the appropriate pathway.

Application engineering

Controlled application determines field performance.

1

Prepare

Glass must be clean, dry and free from surfactants, grease, silicone residues, oils and atmospheric contamination.

2

Apply

Use pathway-specific methods and coverage rates. Quartz supports HVLP spray or wipe application; Titan is applied through controlled wipe/polish procedures.

3

Cure

Both pathways require controlled cure handling. Titan also requires UV exposure for full active effect development.

4

Verify

Compare treated versus control areas and evaluate cleaning, yield and O&M effect using defined monitoring logic.
