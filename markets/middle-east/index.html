GCC O&M pain points

Dust and water logistics define the first review path.

Dust storms

Frequent airborne dust and sand deposition can increase cleaning pressure and yield uncertainty.

Water logistics

Water access, transport, quality and cost often become material O&M constraints.

Robotic cleaning

Compatibility with robotic or semi-automated cleaning should be reviewed with surface recovery and abrasion exposure.

Abrasion exposure

High-frequency cleaning can raise surface-risk concerns where dust and sand are persistent.
