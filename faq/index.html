Technical FAQ

SolarEX FAQ — Technical Questions, Precise Answers

Two distinct surface-engineering pathways. Precise mechanism selection. Evidence-led deployment. Use this guide to identify the correct technical, commercial or application pathway before reaching out.

Start with mechanism

Quartz and Titan are distinct pathways.

SolarEX Quartz — passive SiO₂ pathway

A transparent SiO₂ film engineered for passive hydrophobic and oleophobic surface behavior. No UV activation is required. Quartz functions through surface energy modification, reducing adhesion of dust and non-organic particulates.

SolarEX Titan — active TiO₂ pathway

A TiO₂ coating that activates a photocatalytic mechanism under UV exposure. UV energy triggers reactive oxygen species that decompose organic, biological and industrial surface contamination.

Quartz FAQ

SolarEX Quartz — frequently asked questions.

What is SolarEX Quartz?

A silicon dioxide surface coating applied as a transparent 100–150 nm thin film. It modifies surface energy to deliver hydrophobic and oleophobic behavior.

Does Quartz require UV?

No. Quartz is a passive pathway and is independent of UV availability, making it suitable for high-latitude or diffuse-light environments.

What contaminants is Quartz best suited for?

Inorganic particulates: wind-driven dust, pollen, mineral deposits, salt and airborne particulate matter.

Does Quartz eliminate cleaning?

No. Quartz reduces adhesion and supports easier cleaning, but it does not replace scheduled O&M planning.

Titan FAQ

SolarEX Titan — frequently asked questions.

What is SolarEX Titan?

A TiO₂ photocatalytic coating formulated to decompose organic, biological and industrial contamination through a UV-triggered mechanism.

Why does Titan require UV?

UV photons excite electrons in titanium dioxide, generating reactive oxygen species at the coated surface. Without sufficient UV flux, the active mechanism cannot operate as intended.

What contaminants is Titan best suited for?

Organic matter, biological growth, algae, biofilm, mold spores and industrial atmospheric deposition with organic compounds.

What does hydrophilic behavior mean?

Under UV exposure, Titan-coated surfaces allow water to spread in a thin, even sheet rather than forming discrete droplets.
