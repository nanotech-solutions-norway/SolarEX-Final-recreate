Core distinction

Quartz and Titan are distinct pathways.

What is SolarEX Quartz?

SolarEX Quartz is the passive SiO₂ pathway. It forms a transparent 100–150 nm silicon dioxide nano-film that modifies surface energy to reduce adhesion of dust, pollen, salt, bird lime, mineral particulates and grime.

  • No UV activation required.
  • Hydrophobic and oleophobic behavior.
  • Best fit: inorganic or mineral-dominated soiling.
Open Quartz

What is SolarEX Titan?

SolarEX Titan is the active TiO₂ pathway. It uses titanium dioxide photocatalytic surface chemistry that activates under UV exposure and supports organic contaminant decomposition and hydrophilic rinse behavior.

  • UV activation required.
  • Reactive oxygen species drive active decomposition.
  • Best fit: organic, biological and atmospheric contamination.

Quartz FAQ

SolarEX Quartz — frequently asked questions.

Does Quartz require sunlight or UV to work?

No. Quartz is a passive SiO₂ pathway. Its hydrophobic and oleophobic behavior comes from surface-energy modification and does not require UV activation.

What film thickness does Quartz form?

SolarEX source material states a transparent SiO₂ nano-film of approximately 100–150 nm.

What is the typical application consumption?

Source guidance lists manual consumption around 15–17 mL/m² and industrial consumption around 15–20 mL/m².

What application temperature is preferred?

The source application guidance identifies 15–20°C as the ideal range and cautions against application above 30°C surface temperature.

Does Quartz eliminate cleaning?

No. Quartz reduces adhesion and supports easier cleaning, but it does not replace scheduled O&M planning or site-specific cleaning requirements.

When is Quartz typically preferred?

Quartz is typically preferred when inorganic or mineral soiling dominates, UV availability is low or seasonal, and the objective is easier cleaning and reduced adhesion.

Titan FAQ

SolarEX Titan — frequently asked questions.

Why does Titan require UV?

UV photons activate TiO₂ photocatalysis. The activated surface generates reactive oxygen species that support decomposition of organic, biological and atmospheric contamination.

What does hydrophilic behavior mean?

Under UV activation, Titan supports water spreading as a thin sheet instead of forming discrete droplets. This rinse behavior helps remove loosened contamination.

What is the typical Titan coverage?

Source guidance lists typical coverage around 10–25 mL/m², with a wipe/polish range around 10–15 mL/m² where relevant.

What are the cure and activation timings?

Titan has a full cure of approximately 24 hours. Full outdoor effect develops over approximately 24–48 hours under sunlight depending on climate and UV availability.

When is Titan typically preferred?

Titan is preferred where UV availability is sufficient and the contamination profile includes organic films, biological fouling, smog-derived residues or industrial atmospheric contamination.

Should Titan be used for low-UV mineral soiling sites?

Not by default. If the dominant issue is low-UV mineral dust, Quartz should be assessed first because its passive mechanism does not require UV activation.

Application FAQ

Application questions.

Why is surface preparation critical?

Both products require a clean, dry and residue-free surface. Grease, silicone residues, surfactants, tensides and atmospheric deposits can compromise bonding and invalidate treated/control evaluation.

Can SolarEX be applied to dirty panels?

No. The surface must be cleaned, rinsed where required, dried and prepared according to pathway-specific instructions before application.

What should be recorded during application?

Product route, coverage, temperature, surface condition, method, applicator, batch context, date/time, curing window and weather/UV context should be recorded.

Pilot FAQ

Pilot validation questions.

What should a pilot measure?

Baseline yield, treated/control yield, cleaning log, water use, labor input, contamination profile, rainfall and UV/irradiance context.

How should controls be selected?

Use comparable modules or strings with similar age, tilt, orientation, module type and exposure profile.

What monitoring interval is preferred?

15-minute interval monitoring is preferred where available. Longer monitoring gives stronger seasonal representativeness.

What result is actionable?

A result becomes actionable when treated/control comparison, cleaning impact and commercial value are clear enough for scale-up or further review.
