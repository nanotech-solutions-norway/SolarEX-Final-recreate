Mechanism

The passive pathway for soiling adhesion control.

Quartz is engineered for environments where passive surface repellence is the technically appropriate solution. It requires no UV activation, no photocatalytic trigger and no on-site energy input to function.

No UV activation required

Quartz delivers passive surface repellence from point of cure. UV irradiance is not a prerequisite, making it viable at any latitude and in diffuse-light environments.

Hydrophobic response

Water beads on the coated surface and is more likely to roll off, helping remove loosely held particulates under rain or cleaning action.

Oleophobic response

The surface reduces adhesion of oily grime and environmental residues that contribute to optical-interface contamination.

Easy-clean logic

Quartz supports faster, less abrasive surface recovery within a controlled O&M plan.

Application protocol

Quartz application workflow from source instructions.

Quartz performance depends on surface preparation, residue removal, coverage control, temperature discipline and cure handling.

1

Clean and decontaminate

Clean the PV glass thoroughly. Remove heavy contaminants, insect remains, traffic film, grease, silicone residues, surfactants and tensides. Basic wipe-down is not sufficient for reliable bonding.

2

Rinse and dry

Rinse with clean water where required and dry fully. The glass surface must be clean, dry and residue-free before coating.

3

Final wipe and controlled application

Perform a final alcohol-compatible wipe. Apply by HVLP spray or pre-impregnated wipe/lint-free cloth according to the selected process.

4

Cure and protect

Allow the coating to cure. Full cure is approximately 24 hours; source framing states rain tolerance after approximately 1 hour under relevant conditions.
