Surface engineering

The passive pathway for soiling control.

Quartz is engineered for environments where passive surface repellence is the technically appropriate solution. It requires no UV activation, no photocatalytic trigger and no on-site energy input to function.

No UV activation required

Quartz delivers passive surface repellence from point of cure. UV irradiance is not a prerequisite, making it viable at any latitude and in diffuse-light environments.

Hydrophobic + oleophobic behavior

The SiO₂ nano-film imparts water-repellent and oil-repellent properties. Water beads and rolls off; oily particulates and grime show reduced adhesion energy.

Reduced cleaning burden

Lower adhesion energy supports longer cleaning intervals, faster cleaning and lower wash-water and labor input per cleaning cycle.

Operating fit

Where Quartz performs best.

Dust, pollen and mineral soiling

Optimized for inorganic and mineral-particulate soiling profiles, including airborne dust, pollen and calcium/mineral deposit accumulation.

Low-UV / high-latitude sites

Valuable where UV-dependent photocatalytic activation is less consistent, including Nordic, UK, Scottish and overcast-climate installations.

Rainfall-assisted cleaning

Reduced adhesion allows rain events to carry away loosely held particulates more effectively than untreated glass.

Water-scarce sites

Supports extended cleaning intervals and reduced O&M mobilization cost where water and labor are constrained.
